Terms and Conditions

1. General Provisions: The florist provides services in preparing original floral arrangements from fresh and dried plants, bypassing repetitive, catalog patterns.

2. Seasonality and Substitutions: Due to natural seasonality, we reserve the right to replace individual flower species with others, maintaining color, form, and value, in case of unavailability of selected varieties at the market.

3. Special Orders: Orders for wedding bouquets and larger events should be placed at least two weeks in advance to guarantee the availability of preferred plant species.

4. Complaints: Complaints regarding the freshness of cut flowers are accepted in person or by phone within 24 hours of receiving or delivering the arrangement.
