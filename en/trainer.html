About me

Tomasz Bartczak

Certified Personal Trainer & Sports Nutritionist

The best plan is the one you can sustain for years, not for three weeks.

For seven years, I've worked with people starting from scratch, returning after injuries, or tired of another "Monday revolution". I train in a private studio in Poznań, where I work with one person at a time. Every session starts with a conversation and a short movement test, and only then do I choose weights, range, and tempo. We set up nutrition together – without excluding entire food groups.
