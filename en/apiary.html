The apiary that started with three hives behind the barn

In 1974, Stanisław, Włodek's grandfather, received three hives from a neighbor and one piece of advice: don't look into them too often. They stood under an old pear tree in a village on the edge of Roztocze, and the honey was given to family for the holidays.

Włodek took over the apiary in 1998. He moved the hives to the edge of a fir forest, where linden trees and flower meadows grow, because that's where the bees gather what we like most: linden in July and honeydew in August. Today we have thirty-eight bee colonies and a bottling plant in the old barn.

Halina bottles and labels, Marta runs the store and replies to messages, and Włodek still looks into the hives more often than his grandfather advised.

We do not heat the honey and do not mix harvests from different years. If last year's jar has crystallized, that's good. That's how it should be.

What in which season

We don't have every honey all year round. Bees have their calendar and we stick to it.

April and May
Spring hive inspections. Bees start with willow and dandelion, then the orchards bloom. There's no honey yet, but there is the first pollen, so we set up pollen traps.
June
We spin multiflorous honey from meadows and orchards. Each batch tastes different, depending on what was blooming at the time.
July
Linden trees bloom for less than two weeks. If the weather is good, we harvest linden honey in the second half of the month.
August
Honeydew from firs and spruces. Not every year is a honeydew year, so this honey is scarce and disappears quickly.
September and October
We close the season: we bottle the last batches, collect propolis from the frames, and feed the colonies before winter.
Winter
The bees stay in a winter cluster, and we pack sets and reply to messages. See sets

Frequently asked questions

How do you pack the jars?

Each jar goes into a cardboard insert with wood wool, and we mark the package as glass. If something breaks anyway, we'll send a new jar, just send a picture of the package.

When do you ship orders?

On Mondays and Thursdays, by courier or to a parcel locker. The package usually takes one or two business days. Orders over 150 PLN are shipped for free.

The honey has crystallized. Is it spoiled?

No, crystallization is natural and means the honey hasn't been heated. If you prefer liquid, place the closed jar in warm water, but no higher than 40°C, and wait a dozen minutes.

Can I pick up my order at the apiary?

Yes, on Saturdays from 9:00 to 13:00, by prior appointment. Choose personal pickup when placing your order, and Marta will get in touch with a confirmation.
